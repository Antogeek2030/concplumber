Hard water and age take a toll on water heaters across Concord and Contra Costa County. Catching problems early can save you from a full replacement and protect your floors from water damage.

## 1. No Hot Water or Lukewarm Water

Complete loss of hot water is the most obvious sign. On electric tanks this often means a failed heating element or thermostat. On gas units it can be a pilot outage or thermocouple. Either way, same-day diagnosis usually restores comfort quickly.

## 2. Water Pooling Around the Unit

Any leak around the base of the tank is urgent. It may be a loose fitting, a failing T&P valve, or the start of tank corrosion. Act before the leak spreads under flooring.

## 3. Rumbling, Popping, or Banging Noises

Sediment buildup is common with Concord’s water conditions. When minerals settle at the bottom and the burner fires, you hear rumbling. A professional flush and anode inspection can extend the life of the tank significantly.

## 4. Rusty or Discolored Hot Water

Rusty water from the hot side often points to a depleted anode rod or internal tank corrosion. Replacing the anode early can prevent a full tank failure.

## 5. Inconsistent Temperature

Water that swings from scalding to cold usually means a failing thermostat, sediment affecting the dip tube, or an undersized unit for the household demand.
