Choosing between a traditional tank and a tankless water heater is one of the biggest decisions Concord homeowners make when replacing a unit.

## Traditional Tank Water Heaters

Most Concord homes still use 40–50 gallon gas or electric tanks. They are reliable, relatively inexpensive to install, and familiar to every plumber. Drawbacks include standby heat loss and a finite supply of hot water during peak demand.

Hard water in parts of the East Bay accelerates sediment buildup, so annual flushing and anode checks are important.

## Tankless (On-Demand) Systems

Tankless units heat water only when you need it. They take up less space, last longer when maintained, and can lower energy bills. Popular brands in our area include Rinnai, Navien, and Rheem.

They require proper gas line sizing or electrical capacity and regular descaling because of local mineral content. Installation cost is higher, but many households recover the difference over time through efficiency.

## Which Is Right for You?

- **Keep a tank** if you want the lowest upfront cost and have space for a standard unit.
- **Go tankless** if you value continuous hot water, lower long-term energy use, and can accommodate the venting and gas/electric requirements.
